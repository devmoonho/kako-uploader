Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

1. Information received. When the operator connects a TikTok account, TikTok provides basic account information (open ID, display name, profile image) and access tokens for the permissions user.info.basic, video.upload and video.publish.

2. How it is used. This information is used only to upload videos to that TikTok account and to check the upload status.

3. Storage. Tokens are stored only on the operator's own computer. There is no server, and no information is shared with or sold to anyone.

4. Other people. The tool does not collect any information about other TikTok users or viewers.

5. Retention and deletion. The operator can delete the stored tokens at any time and can disconnect the app in TikTok settings (Settings and privacy > Security > Apps and services). Deleted tokens cannot be used again.
